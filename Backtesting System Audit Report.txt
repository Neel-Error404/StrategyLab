# Backtesting System Audit — Concise Report (for quick hand-off)

*Scope:* Align backtest vs live for a multi-timeframe MACD/EMA strategy; remove biases; explain mismatches; lock a reproducible comparison protocol.&#x20;

---

## 0) TL;DR

* **Why trades didn’t match:** (a) **Warm-up too short** in backtest, (b) **signal timing** used the current bar instead of the **previous bar** in places, (c) **entries/exits at the same bar** (no **two-bar rule**), (d) **cascade-prevention** active in backtest but **not mirrored in live**, (e) minor **quantity/rounding/cost** and **EOD** handling differences.
* **Fixes applied:** **525-minute warm-up**, strict **previous-bar** indicators, **next-bar open** execution for entries/exits, and config unification.
* **Result:** Live trades now behave as an **(almost) subset** of backtest trades; residual deltas are explainable (rounding, fills).
* **Do next:** Standardize configs, make **one strategy codepath** for both modes, add **EOD auto-exit**, and ship a **compare-run** that asserts the subset rule daily.

---

## 1) Root causes ? concrete fixes

### A. Warm-up & indicator stability

* **Problem:** Backtest skipped \~30 min vs live \~390 min ? unstable 15-min MACD/EMA early bars ? **spurious backtest trades**.
* **Fix:** Use **525 min (35×15-min bars)** warm-up everywhere.
* **Effect:** Early false trades vanish; trade counts converge.

### B. Multi-timeframe lookahead risk

* **Problem:** Risk of using **incomplete higher-TF candles** mid-bar.
* **Fix:** Build 5m/15m from 1m; **merge\_asof (backward)** + **use previous bar** (`shift(1)`) in signals.
* **Effect:** No future leakage; signals only on **closed** HTF bars.

### C. Trade timing (two-bar rule)

* **Problem:** Some backtests **entered/exited on the same bar** as detection ? optimistic fills.
* **Fix:** When a signal prints at bar close **t**, **execute at t+1 open** (entry/exit-pending flags).
* **Effect:** Realistic pricing; live/backtest sequencing matches.

### D. Cascade-prevention mismatch

* **Problem:** Backtest blocks **same-direction re-entry** in a day; live didn’t always.
* **Fix (choose 1):** **Enable** same rule in live **or** **disable** in backtest; keep it **parametrized** & logged.
* **Effect:** Identical opportunity set across modes.

### E. Minor—but impactful—operational gaps

* **Quantities/rounding:** Align lot/qty rounding & fractional handling.
* **Costs/slippage:** Same brokerage, taxes, slippage % in both modes.
* **EOD behavior:** Force **auto-square-off at 15:30** in backtest if a position is open (mark exit at next bar’s open or last tradeable price).
* **Timezone/timestamps:** Keep **IST**, candles timestamped at **bar end** in both systems.
* **Data parity:** Prefer a **single historical source** (or record live 1-min bars and replay them).

## 2) Strategy logic (kept intact, now made consistent)

* **Entry (Long/Short):** **4-point alignment** on **5m & 15m** (MACD line >/< signal **and** EMA9 >/< EMA20) using **previous bar** values.
* **Exit:** 15m **MACD-hist retrace** — track extreme since entry; exit when hist retraces **=80%** (i.e., falls below **20% of peak** for longs; rises above **20% of trough** for shorts).
* **Execution:** **Next-bar open** for both entries and exits.
* **Optional guard:** **Cascade-prevention** (configurable).

---

## 3) Why live 11 vs backtest 16 with only 3 overlapping (diagnosis mapping)

| Symptom                          | Likely cause           | Resolution                         |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Extra early trades in backtest   | **Short warm-up**      | **525-min** warm-up                |
| Misaligned timestamps/signals    | **Current-bar** usage  | Strict **previous-bar** indicators |
| Entry/exit at unrealistic prices | No **two-bar rule**    | **Execute next-bar open**          |
| Different day re-entries         | **Cascade** in BT only | Mirror setting in **both**         |
| Small price/qty deltas           | **Rounding/costs/EOD** | Align **qty/costs**; **EOD close** |

After fixes, **backtest = live** trades with **live ? backtest** holding in audits, barring fills/rounding edge cases.

## 4) Standard baseline (lock these in config)

* **Timeframes:** Base **1m**, signals on **5m & 15m**.
* **Warm-up:** **525 min** minimum.
* **Signal data:** **shift(1)** (strict prev-bar).
* **Execution price:** **next-bar open** (entries & exits).
* **Cascade:** **ON/OFF** — but **same** in both modes.
* **Costs:** Brokerage, taxes, slippage **identical**.
* **EOD:** Force flat by **15:30 IST** in sim.
* **Timezone:** **IST**, candles stamped at **bar end**.
* **Data:** Prefer **same source** or **live-recorded** 1-min.

---

## 5) Daily “subset check” (operational playbook)

1. **Record live** trades & 1-min bars (all symbols).
2. **Replay backtest** on the **exact live window**, same config commit.
3. **Pair trades** (ticker, side, timestamp bucket ±1 bar).
4. **Assert subset:** Every live trade must have a matching backtest trade; log **non-matches** with reason code:

   * WARMUP, TIMING, CASCADE, EOD, QTY, COST, DATA\_GAP.
5. **Fail the run** if reason ? {QTY,COST} — investigate.

---

## 6) What still won’t be pixel-perfect (acceptable drift)

* **Open-to-open gaps** around entries/exits (rare on large caps).
* **Broker fill micro-slippage** vs fixed slippage model.
* **Transient live data hiccups** (missed minute) — flag via heartbeat and annotate the compare.

---

## 7) Hardening & housekeeping

* **One codepath:** Extract strategy into a **shared module** used by **both** backtest & live.
* **Parameters not branches:** Pass **exit-threshold** & **cascade** via config instead of multiple classes.
* **Auto-tests:** Lint for **no current-bar reads**, enforce **two-bar rule**, **warm-up = max span**.
* **Resampling clarity:** Explicit `label='right', closed='right'` and doc on bar semantics.
* **Debug artifacts:** Optional dump of a few joined 1m rows showing 5m/15m forward-filled columns for eyeballing.
